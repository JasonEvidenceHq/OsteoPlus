About Us

Osteopathic treatment with a clear plan and a practical focus.

Justin Barr is the owner of Osteo+. The clinic is built around careful assessment, hands-on treatment and useful advice for people who want to understand their pain and move more comfortably.

Treatment approach

Hands-on care, movement advice and time to ask questions.

Osteopathic principles and treatment form the foundation of the clinic. Depending on your needs, treatment may involve spinal manipulative therapy, muscle energy techniques, acupuncture, ultrasound, shortwave diathermy and other electrotherapy approaches.

The aim is to reduce irritation where possible, improve range of motion and give you practical advice that makes sense outside the treatment room.

Ease pain and stiffness

Support for back pain, neck pain, joint pain, headaches and movement-related discomfort.

Improve movement

Work on posture, flexibility, mobility and range of motion in the areas that matter to you.

Reduce recurrence

Advice and exercises to help you reduce strain and manage the factors that may keep symptoms returning.

Recover from activity

Care for sports injuries and active lifestyles, with a focus on returning steadily and confidently.

Appointments

What happens when you visit.

1

Talk through the problem

Start with symptoms, history, goals and anything that changes or aggravates the issue.

2

Assess movement

Check how the affected area and related joints are moving before agreeing a suitable treatment plan.

3

Treat and advise

Use appropriate hands-on treatment, supported by clear advice for work, sport, exercise and recovery.

Common questions

Helpful details before you book.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is usually needed. You can book directly online or call the clinic.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothing that lets you move easily for assessment and treatment.

Can I ask questions during treatment?

Yes. The appointment should feel clear, collaborative and easy to understand.
