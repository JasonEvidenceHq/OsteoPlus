How treatment works

Choose the route that fits your body and your goals.

The best osteopathy clinic sites make treatment feel specific rather than mysterious: clear diagnosis, personalised hands-on care, movement advice and a steady path back to normal life. This page brings that approach together for everyday pain, sports injuries, recovery work and focused soft tissue treatment.

01

Osteopathy

Osteopathy is hands-on musculoskeletal care for the way your joints, muscles, ligaments and connective tissues work together. At Osteo+, treatment starts with assessment and a clear explanation before any hands-on work begins.

  • Back pain, neck pain and joint stiffness
  • Muscle tension, headaches related to neck strain and everyday aches
  • Postural issues, reduced mobility and recurring discomfort

02

Sports Osteopathy

Sports osteopathy supports people who want to stay active, train well and recover from movement-related injuries. The focus is on understanding what has been overloaded, what needs to calm down and what needs to become stronger or more mobile.

  • Running, gym, racket sport and weekend activity injuries
  • Muscle strains, ligament sprains and overuse irritation
  • Return-to-activity advice that matches your sport and routine
Book sports care online
03

Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation turns treatment into progress between appointments. Your plan may include mobility work, strength exercises, postural advice and simple progressions that build confidence without overwhelming your week.

  • Targeted exercises for strength, control and joint stability
  • Recovery plans after injury, flare-ups or surgery where appropriate
  • Advice to reduce the risk of symptoms returning
Book rehabilitation advice
04

OsteoMassage

OsteoMassage is focused soft tissue treatment with an osteopathic eye. It is designed for muscular tightness, postural strain and activity-related soreness, with treatment guided by how your body moves rather than by relaxation alone.

  • Deep tissue and remedial soft tissue techniques
  • Muscle tension from training, work, driving or desk posture
  • A useful complement to osteopathy and rehabilitation

Which treatment is right?

You do not need to diagnose yourself before booking.

If you are unsure what to choose, book the option that feels closest to your main goal. Your appointment will begin with assessment, and the treatment approach can be adapted to your symptoms, preferences and comfort.

Choose Osteopathy if...

You want a full assessment for pain, stiffness, posture, mobility or a problem that keeps returning.

Choose Sports Osteopathy if...

Your symptoms are linked to training, sport, exercise load or returning to activity after injury.

Choose Rehabilitation if...

You need structured exercises and progression to rebuild strength, control and confidence.

Choose OsteoMassage if...

You want focused soft tissue work for tight, tired or overloaded muscles.
